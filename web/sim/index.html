TIPHold ARP (9, or Shift-click it to keep it held) and turn any knob: a help card says what that knob does, on every page. The value does not change. Turn the same knob further to scroll, let go of ARP to close it.

Try the generators

Switch on first. It starts with STILL TIDE, the first demo in the video: an ambient piece made to run for an hour, a breathing FM6 drone, a TRIO pad on AWAKE, wind chimes on FALL and a sub bass on MARKOV, thinned and filled by ENERGY. The button plays it from the top (or press PLAY); KOTO GARDEN is in slot B (SAVE > PROJECT).

The demo is loaded as a project, as on the device, so PLAY plays it. To start from an empty project: tap SAVE (8) until the PROJECT page, turn KNOB 1 down to New song and press OCT+ (]): the demo stops. OCT+ again for Yes to "Start a new song?", then pick the key (KNOB 1 to 3), OCT+, a role per track (KNOB 1 to 4), OCT+: every pattern is empty. OCT- ([) goes back.

Or set one up yourself, on the selected track (T1 when you switch on):

  1. Press SEQ (0). The screen shows the GEN page, with MODE on KNOB 1 (on the demo's track 1, CHORD).
  2. Turn KNOB 1 (MODE): STEP, ARP, then the generators, CHORD, TURING, EUCLID, MARKOV, MORPH and on. STEP is the normal step sequencer.
  3. Press PLAY (-), then turn KNOB 2 to 4 while it plays.
  4. Turn ALGORITHM (← →) to pick another track and give it a generator too: it joins in time with the one playing. PRESETS changes its sound (on a page other than HOME, hold GLO while turning it).

TURING

A looping shift register: a riff that repeats or slowly mutates.

LENGTH
loop length, 2 to 16 steps
PROB
chance of a change each step; at 0 (Lock) it repeats exactly
SPAN
pitch range, 1 to 3 octaves

EUCLID

Hits spread as evenly as possible over the loop.

STEPS
loop length, 1 to 32
PULSE
number of hits
ROT
shifts the pattern round the loop

MARKOV

A melody that wanders from note to note.

LEAP
small steps (low) or big jumps (high)
DENS
chance each step plays; try 60 to 70 %
SPAN
pitch range in octaves

MORPH

Blends two rhythm patterns.

POS
from pattern A (0) to pattern B (127)
SNP A
pattern A, one of 8
SNP B
pattern B, one of 8

Make it change over time: from GEN, turn SELECT on to EVOLVE. EVERY sets how many bars pass between changes, AMOUNT how big they are, and ROLL makes one change now. The next pages are FORM, which builds sections (AB, ABAC and more) and returns to them, and TWEAK (SHIFT, DROP, HUMAN, FOLLOW). Notes snap to the track's scale: press SCL to change it.
