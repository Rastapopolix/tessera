Tessera is firmware for the FM-1 by Theo Dainis (Rastapopolix), a fork of Melodee: it adds the Tessera generators and a project format of its own. github.com/Rastapopolix/tessera

    Melodee by Ellic Studio, based on Felucca
    by Leo Kuroshita (@kurogedelic), Hügelton Instruments, with its CrispyZebra waveforms and Fukiai icons (MIT).

    Font: Inter Tight (The Inter Project Authors, SIL OFL 1.1). Samples: Versilian Studios VSCO-2 Community Edition and VCSL (CC0 1.0).

    FM6 engine: msfa from Dexed (Google Inc., Pascal Gauthier, Apache-2.0). The package also holds three JieLi AC79 SDK files, uboot.boot, cfg_tool.bin and eq_cfg_hw.bin (JieLi Technology, Apache-2.0). Licences: GPL-3.0, Apache-2.0, all.
