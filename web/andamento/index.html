Encoders

Drag up or down, or use the mouse wheel. A click (without dragging) pushes it: the setting goes back to its default.

Encoders A to H follow the 2×4 grid on the screen; on HOME they are tracks 1 to 8's ENERGY DEPTH. ENERGY works on every page; VOL is the main level.

HOME

Where Andamento starts, and where NO brings you back from any page: the piece at a glance. The header names the piece's SHAPE (FREE when PIECE has no LENGTH) and its time, or REST and its seconds while the notes rest.

Under it ENERGY over time, full above the dotted line and sparse below: with a LENGTH the whole piece across the screen, the plan dim and what played lit up to the play head; FREE it grows from the left from PLAY and then scrolls, the last three minutes. Marks under the curve are rests, ticks above it where Entries brought a track in or out. Along the foot the 8 tracks: a meter that fills as a track is in and lights while it sounds, its DEPTH underneath. Tracks 5 to 8 are empty until the 8 tracks arrive.

Buttons

A page button opens that page; pressed again it goes to its second page (or ↑ ↓).

← → choose the track; FUNC held with key 1 to 4 picks one directly. PLAY restarts while playing.

The 16 keys

Hold FUNC and press a top-row key for the keys' mode: 9 SCALE (16 degrees of the scale from the root), 10 CHROM (a keyboard: white keys below, their sharps above), 11 STEP (the track's pattern, 16 steps at a time), 12 MUTE (top row mutes, bottom row selects).

FUNC with ↑ ↓: the octave, or the next 16 steps. The lights: the track's colour, a flash where it plays (its generator too), the step playing white, red for a muted track; turning ENERGY shows its level across the keys.

Computer keyboard

Keys: A…K bottom row, Q…I top row. Pages 1…6.

Space PLAY / STOP, Shift FUNC, Esc NO (HOME), arrows as on the panel. The modes: Shift with Q W E R.
