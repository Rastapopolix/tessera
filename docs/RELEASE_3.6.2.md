**Tessera 3.6.2** follows [Tessera 3.6.1](https://github.com/Rastapopolix/tessera/releases/tag/v3.6.1): LANDSCAPE becomes a page to play as well as watch, with its knobs turning PERF's macros, and its night sky and shores are fixed. Everything in the [3.6](https://github.com/Rastapopolix/tessera/releases/tag/v3.6) and [3.6.1](https://github.com/Rastapopolix/tessera/releases/tag/v3.6.1) notes still applies.

### 🎛️ Performing
* **LANDSCAPE's knobs play the MACROs**: KNOB 1 to 4 on GLO > LANDSCAPE turn **MACRO 1 to 4**, as on PERF, so you can thin the music or make a big move and watch the lake answer. A macro being turned shows large across the foot (there are no columns on LANDSCAPE, so whatever LARGE VALUE is set to), and the screen reader says it. With **IDLE LANDSCAPE**, the first touch, a knob too, still only takes you back to your page: a bump never changes the music.

### 🖥️ On the screen
* **LANDSCAPE at night**: a few stars stay out however busy the music, where before they all vanished at about four notes a second; the next ones come and go dimly as it thins and fills.
* **LANDSCAPE's shores**: when a CHAIN section's new shore comes in, the two shores now run into each other where they meet, instead of meeting at a hard edge. Steep slopes are drawn with a solid outline, as cliffs.

### 📦 Installation
Open **[rastapopolix.github.io/tessera](https://rastapopolix.github.io/tessera/)** in Chrome or Edge, connect the FM-1 by USB and press **Install**, then keep the tab open and the FM-1 connected until the install window says Done. The installer can also put the official M-VAVE firmware back. Or take `tessera-3.6.2.fwsc` (dual core) or `tessera-3.6.2-1core.fwsc` from this release.

### ⚠️ Good to know
* **Projects load and play the same as on 3.6.1**, and a project saved on 3.6.2 loads on 3.6.1 and 3.6 as it did from 3.6.1 (nothing in the project format changed). As with 3.6, a project saved on 3.6 or later does not load on 3.5.3 or older.
* The identity stays **FM-1_836**: M-UPGRADE 3.11 and later recognise it, and the web installer offers 3.6.2 as an update.
