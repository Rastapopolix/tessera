**Tessera 3.7.3** follows [Tessera 3.7.2](https://github.com/Rastapopolix/tessera/releases/tag/v3.7.2): one fix, for ANSWER's chords. Everything in the [3.7](https://github.com/Rastapopolix/tessera/releases/tag/v3.7), [3.7.1](https://github.com/Rastapopolix/tessera/releases/tag/v3.7.1) and [3.7.2](https://github.com/Rastapopolix/tessera/releases/tag/v3.7.2) notes still applies.

### 🔧 Fixed
* **ANSWER CHORD TRUE answers chords as chords again.** With the keys held (as they always are on the FM-1), a chord in the call came back as its first note only. Now every note of the chord is answered together, held as long as the chord was.

### 📦 Installation
Open **[rastapopolix.github.io/tessera](https://rastapopolix.github.io/tessera/)** in Chrome or Edge, connect the FM-1 by USB and press **Install**, then keep the tab open and the FM-1 connected until the install window says Done. The installer can also put the official M-VAVE firmware back. Or take `tessera-3.7.3.fwsc` (dual core) or `tessera-3.7.3-1core.fwsc` from this release.

### ⚠️ Good to know
* **Projects from 3.7.2 load and play the same**, and nothing in the project format changed: a 3.7.3 project loads on 3.7.2 as before.
* The identity stays **FM-1_837**: M-UPGRADE 3.11 and later recognise it, and the web installer offers 3.7.3 as an update.
