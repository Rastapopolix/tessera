Tessera shortcuts

A + B: hold A, then press or turn B. Held: about 0.7 s (the GLO and SCL layers: MENU > HOLD, 0.3 to 0.6 s); ARP, FX, LFO and OCT‑ on GEN work as soon as they are down. Page buttons act when let go. KNOB 1 to 4 are the four knobs from the left.

Getting around

HOMEThe home page: four tracks, their sounds and knobs
HOME heldThe menu (held again: leaves it)
ALGORITHMPicks the track, on every page (MENU > TRACK KNOB LEFT swaps it with PRESETS)
SELECTThe pages of the section shown, both ways; on HOME and MIXER the tempo
A page button againThat section's next page (from elsewhere most return to the page last used)
OCT‑ (off HOME)Back: closes a list, sheet, map or selection; a page opened from the map or MATRIX: back there; else nothing (HOME: the home page)
OCT+ (off HOME)Enter: opens the row, slot, note or mod picked; does the action picked; else nothing (the sheet: held)
OCT+ heldThe page's sheet: the sound's (HOME, EDIT, ENV, LFO, FX), the pattern's (GEN, NOTES, PATTERN; Swing there), the song's (SONG), the pattern's on PATTERNS; a generator's (GEN: QUAD, CONSTL, ATLAS, ANSWER, CHORD), MIDI OUT's, ENTRIES', MODS'; elsewhere NO SHEET
A sheet: KNOB 1, OCT+ / OCT‑Picks the row / does it / closes
A dialog: OCT+ / OCT‑Yes / no
Lists (menu, SCL, VOICE): KNOB 1 / KNOB 2The row / its value
MENU > GROUNDWhat the ink is drawn on: DARK (black, the default) or LIGHT (paper, each ink's light version)
MENU > LARGE VALUEThe knob turning, large at the foot: AUTO (pages without a picture), ON, OFF

The page buttons

SEQWhat plays: GEN, then STEP: NOTES (a DRUM track: NOTES, DRUM HIT). ARP: NOTES, ARP. A generator: PATTERN. All: TEMPO, PATTERNS, SONG
SEQ heldPATTERNS
ARPHow it is shaped. STEP: EVOLVE, TWEAK, TIMING. ARP: TWEAK (SWING, PROB, HOLD, ORDER: ARP with OCT‑ held). A generator: EVOLVE, FORM, TWEAK, TWEAK 2, LENGTH, TIMING, ENERGY
LFOMODS, MATRIX, LFO, LFO 2, ORGANIC, LFO DEST; on FM6 then FM LFO; on ORACLE then LFO MOD, LFO ROUTE
ENVENV, ENV DEST; on FM6 instead EG RATE, EG LVL, PITCH EG, PITCH LV; on ORACLE FILT ENV, AMP ENV
SCLSCALES, SCL, CHORD
SCALES: KNOB 1 to USERYour own tunings: Scala files sent from the web editor (Projects > Tunings)
EDITThe sound's EDIT pages (ORACLE: MACRO, MACRO 2, OSC A, OSC B, OSC MIX, FILTER, POLY MOD, CHARACTER, ...)
EDIT on an EDIT pageThe map of the sound's pages: KNOB 1 across, KNOB 2 rows (on the page last used in each), OCT+ opens (OCT‑ there: the map again), OCT‑ or EDIT closes
PRESETS on an EDIT pageThe next / previous section (FM6: Algo, OP1 to OP6, Pitch, LFO, Ctrl, Store)
FXThe insert and send effects
GLOMIXER, PERF (MACRO 1 to 4, the project's mod sources), PIECE (the piece's LENGTH, SHAPE, END), CHAIN (the project's FORM, PHRASE, WANDER), ENTRIES (tracks out and back in), LANDSCAPE (the music as rain on water), SYSTEM (BUDGET: the shared voices; CPU), MIDI IN, MIDI OUT
On MIXER: KNOB 1 to 4 / with OCT‑ heldLevel / pan of track 1 to 4; on MIXER, PERF and LANDSCAPE the keys are GLO's without holding it
SAVEUSER (save the sound), PROJECT, PRESETS

Quick layers: hold GLO or SCL

GLO held[GLO] SET over the page; tapped twice, [GLO] LOCK (no need to hold; tap again to close)
GLO + black keys F#3 G#3 A#3 C#4Mute / unmute track 1 to 4 (lit: sounding); MENU > MUTES BAR or PHRASE waits for it (blinking)
GLO + white keys F3 G3 A3 B3Solo track 1 to 4 while the key is held
GLO + C4 / F4Unmute all / tap the tempo (three taps or more; INT clock)
GLO + E4FILL, while held (steps set to FILL / !FILL)
GLO + KNOB 1 to 4Level of track 1 to 4
GLO + PRESETSBrowse the selected track's sounds, on any page (the sound shows large in place of the GLO map)
GLO + PLAYRestart from the top without stopping
SCL + any keyROOT: that key's note name (every melodic track while SCL > Key link is LINKED)
SCL + KNOB 1 to 4ROOT, SCALE, CHRD, VOIC
GLO / SCL + OCT‑Puts back what the layer changed since it opened
GLO or SCL tapped twiceLocks the layer open; press it again to close

Knob holds

FX + a knobMod shortcut: a cord from the track's LFO to that parameter, the turn sets its amount
FX + SELECTThe mod shortcut's source
LFO + a knobA momentary change: back as it was when LFO is let go
LFO + a knob, then OCT+Keeps the change ("KEPT")
OCT‑ / OCT+ on HOMEOctave down / up; both together: octave 0

Steps and notes (SEQ > NOTES)

KeysThe cursor step (pressed together: a chord of up to 4); let go: the cursor moves on
A key + SELECTThat note's length
ENV + SELECT or KNOB 1The cursor's note longer / shorter
SCL + SELECT or KNOB 1Moves the note (ties, chord and velocity with it)
EDIT tappedDeletes the note and its ties; the cursor moves on the way KNOB 1 last turned
OCT+The note's sheet (length, velocity, chance, ratchet x1–x8, nudge ±/16, accumulate ±7, condition 1:2..8:8, FILL...); an empty step: NOTHING THERE
OCT+ + SELECT, KNOB 1 or 2Selects a run of notes; let go: the Selected notes sheet (move, copy, quantize, delete)
OCT‑Clears the selection
PRESETSZooms the notes
OCT+ held(PATTERN SHEET) The pattern's sheet: Snap to scale, Double, Half, Euclid (hits, turn), Accum limit / mode, Quantize, Clear

The drum grid (NOTES on a DRUM track)

White keysThe selected lane's hit on that step, on or off
Black keys F#3 to A#4Pick lane 1 to 8
C#5 + white keyThe hit's accent
D#5 / F#5Page down / up (patterns over 16 steps)
KNOB 1 to 4STEP, LANE, HIT, ACC
EDITClears the cursor step
OCT+The hit's sheet (accent, chance, ratchet, nudge, condition); an empty place: NOTHING THERE

Patterns

SEQ + white keys F3 to F4Pattern 1 to 8 (playing: when the pattern playing ends)
SEQ + a pattern key held + anotherCopies the first pattern onto the second
SEQ + SELECTThe next / previous pattern
PATTERNS: KNOB 1 to 4Track 1 to 4's next pattern
PATTERNS: OCT‑Clears the queue
PATTERNS: OCT+ heldThe pattern's sheet: Copy to, Delete pattern
SONG: KNOB 1 to 4Section (+ adds one), track, its pattern there, repeats (1 to 16)
SONG: PLAY or OCT+Plays the song; STOP brings back the patterns you had
SONG: OCT+ heldInsert, duplicate, delete section; take jam; clear song

Recording and transport

PLAYStart / stop; on SONG: plays the song. Generators start from the top of their seed
RECArms the track; records every pass (overdub) until REC again (playing: from the loop's start)
REC + PLAYRecords now
REC heldGenerator track: prints its last bars into STEP. STEP EVOLVE: keeps the changes. STEP: captures what you played
REC held on SONGTake jam: the patterns played since PLAY become the song
EDIT held, recording on NOTESErases as the steps pass
EDIT + REC on NOTESClear pattern? (OCT+), REC stays on for a new take
OCT‑ / OCT+ on NOTES, REC onKeys octave down / up; both: octave 0
EDIT heldUndo (the last load, capture or print; on NOTES the last edit)
EDIT + OCT‑ / OCT+Undo / redo

Saving and projects

SAVE + RECSaves the project to its slot (stops playback first)
SAVEUSER, its Save sheet ready: OCT+ saves the sound
EDIT on USER or PROJECTRenames the slot
OCT+ on a PROJECT slotLoad, Save here, Rename, Boot, Erase
GLO > MIDI OUT: OCT‑ held, KNOB 1 to 4Each track's MIDI OUT TRANSPOSE, a semitone a click
GLO > MIDI OUT: OCT+ heldIts sheet: each track's SOUND (OFF: MIDI only) and DRUM MAP (GM, ROW)
GLO > PIECE, SHAPE USER: OCT‑ held, KNOB 1 / 2Picks one of its eight points / sets its level
GLO > CHAIN: OCT‑ held, KNOB 1 to 4Each place's REPEAT, 1 to 8 phrases
GLO > ENTRIES: OCT‑ held, KNOB 1 to 4Each track's ROLE for ENTRIES: AUTO, BASS, RHYTHM, HARMONY, MELODY, ANCHOR
GLO > ENTRIES: OCT+ heldIts sheet: each track's ORDER: AUTO (by ROLE), 1ST to 4TH (in in that order, out the other way)
GLO: a black key on a track ENTRIES took outBrings it back now, left alone until the next section

Generators

GEN: KNOB 1MODE: STEP, ARP or a generator
GEN: OCT‑ held, KNOB 2 to 4The second layer: TURING, EUCLID, MARKOV, MORPH (SOURCE, KEEP, GRAVITY), QUAD (COL, MOVE, EVERY), CHORD (STRUM 1-250 ms, DIR, OCT), ATLAS (KICK, SNR, HAT), CONSTL (LOCK, LEN, FILL), ANSWER (SOURCE, ORDER, SHIFT)
GEN: OCT+ heldThe GEN sheet: QUAD's RANGE, CONSTL's SIZE, ATLAS' fourth part, ANSWER's SPACE, CHORD's CHRD, VOIC and BREATH / LEN
EVOLVE: OCT‑ heldKNOB 1 ROLL ALL; KNOB 3 PAT GEN; KNOB 4 GRAVITY (a generator) or HOW: DRIFT, ADORN, SERIAL (a STEP track)
TIMING: OCT‑ heldKNOB 1 SPLIT, the chance a step plays at half its division; KNOB 2 HOW: DOUBLE, PUSH, MIX
ATLAS: OCT‑ + a black keyThe fourth part's sound (TOM, CLAP, OPEN HAT, RIM, BELL)
CHORD, PROG USER: OCT‑ + keysOne chord a key, its root the key's note
AWAKE: a white keyPicks that step: KNOB 1 its A, KNOB 2 its B; the key again lets it go
EVOLVE: KNOB 4 (ROLL)A new seed a click; turn back for the last one
GEN, TWEAK: OCT‑ held, KNOB 1RANDOM: new values for the page's knobs; turn back to put them back
FORM: KNOB 3 (CLEAR)Forgets B and C
FORM: OCT‑ held, KNOB 1DEVELOP: each return of a section varies a little (0: exact)
FORM: OCT‑ held, KNOB 2 / 3The CHAIN: FOLLOW or OWN; SKIP, the chance it lags a phrase
FORM: OCT‑ held, KNOB 4ROLE: this track's part for ENTRIES
TWEAK: OCT‑ held, KNOB 2 / 3FOLLOW's HOW (SAME, CHORD, CONTRA, BASS, CANON1 to 8); RHYTHM (OWN, SOURCE, BOTH)
TWEAK: OCT‑ held, KNOB 4FOLLOW's HOLD (shown as T3H2 on FOLLOW)
MODS: OCT+The picked mod: +- (both ways) / + (one way) / GATE
MODS: OCT‑ held, KNOB 4 / KNOB 3A GATE's threshold / a CC mod's CC number
MODS: OCT+ heldMODS' sheet (the picked mod named): Clear mod, then each MIX bus's Mode (SUM, MAX, MIN, DIFF) and Lag
MATRIX: OCT+Opens the picked mod in MODS

MIDI LEARN

MENU > MIDI LEARN: KNOB 2 right or OCT+Learning on (the header says so)
Learning: a knob, then a CC from your controllerThat CC sets the knob's parameter; again for more
Learning: OCT‑Ends it
MENU > MIDI LEARN: KNOB 2 left, then OCT+Forgets every learned CC (OCT‑: keeps them)

Power-on and update

Any button or key at power-onSkips the boot mark
OCT‑ alone while switching onThe update loader (three key-light flashes), to reinstall from the web installer
OCT‑ + OCT+ while switching onHARDWARE CALIBRATION: teach each button and knob
OCT‑ + OCT+ held 5 sUpdate mode (UBOOT); a countdown from 2 s, let go to cancel
