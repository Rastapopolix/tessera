Cosmati quick reference

Ctrl is Cmd on a Mac (Ctrl-click stays the right-click there). Click the window first so the keys reach Cosmati. Shift-drag and Alt-drag are held while you drag.

Sections (keys 1 to 9)

GEN (1)generator, NOTES, ARP, PATTERN, MOTION
SOUND (2)the engine's pages, ENV
MOD (3)MODS, MATRIX, LFOs, ORGANIC
FX (4)the effects
SHAPE (5)EVOLVE, FORM, TWEAK, LENGTH, TIMING, ENERGY
PIECE (6)PIECE, CHAIN, ENTRIES, LANDSCAPE, PATTERNS, SONG
MIX (7)MIXER, PERF
SCALE (8)SCALES, SCL, KEY, CHORD
SETUP (9)SETTINGS, TEMPO, SYSTEM, MIDI IN, MIDI OUT
HOME (0), SAVE (-), REC (=)as on the FM-1

Getting around

A section buttonIts last page; again: the next page, wrapping after the last
Right-click a sectionLatches the FM-1 button it stands for
Footer tab, wheel over the tabsThat page; the wheel turns the pages
Sideways scrollPrevious / next page, stopping at the ends
Ctrl+MThe page map: click any page
Shift+Left / RightPrevious / next section
Up / DownRows of a list (else SELECT)
Shift+Up / DownPrevious / next track
Left / RightALGORITHM
Mouse back / forwardBACK / OK
[ ] or Esc EnterBACK / OK
Page Up / DownOCT+ / OCT‑
TabTracks 1-4 / 5-8
F1, ?Help card of the value under the mouse
Click awayCloses a list, sheet or card (as BACK)

Values

Drag up / down, wheelTurns the value
Double-clickIts default
Shift-drag, Shift-wheelFine steps
Ctrl-clickType it: "-6dB", "1/8", "saw" (Enter sets, Esc closes)
Right-clickHELP, DEFAULT, COPY / PASTE VALUE, RANDOM, TYPE, LOCK
Click a boxed choiceThe next choice
Drag a picture's handleEnvelope corners, filter peak, algorithm operators, ATLAS, CONSTELLATION, MORPH bar
Drag the tempoA BPM a step (double-click: 120); the DAW sets it while playing
Knob ring tick / white arcThe setting / where the mods have it now

Lists and the sound browser

Wheel on a listScrolls a row a notch
Click a rowMoves there; drag changes its value
Double-click a rowMoves there and OK
Click a soundHear it (stopped): root, fifth, octave, root
Double-click a soundKeep it (OK); BACK puts the old one back
Click the sound countFIND: type to filter, Enter keeps, Esc clears
Drag a sound onto a TRACKS rowLoads it on that track (EXPANDED)

Notes and drums

Click a stepA note at the mouse's pitch; again at another height: a chord (up to 4)
Drag a note up / downRe-pitch it; Shift: the whole chord
Drag across empty stepsPaints a run of notes
Drag from a note across stepsErases the notes it crosses
Alt-drag across a noteIts length
Alt-drag up / downIts velocity
Right-click a noteRemoves it
Drum grid: click, right-clickToggle a hit, clear it
Drum grid: drag, drag a hit up / downPaint / erase a lane, accent
CHORD USER: drag a card, +, right-clickMove the root, add a chord, remove it

Mod and long form

MATRIX: click a cellOpens its mod
MATRIX: drag a cell onto anotherMakes a mod (a free slot), opens MODS
MODS: click, drag, double-clickPick, amount, type
PERF: click a MACRO's rowOpens or makes its mod
ENTRIES: click a cellAUTO, IN, OUT, FADE; right-click: AUTO
ENTRIES: click / right-click a letterSets / clears the column
PATTERNS: click a cellQueues that pattern
SONG: click a letter or blockPicks the section / its track

Tracks

Click HOME's column, a TRACKS rowSelects the track
Double-click itIts sounds / its EDIT page
Ctrl+CCopy the track (shared by every Cosmati)
Ctrl+VPaste the track; its mods keep their track numbers
Ctrl+Shift+VPaste only the sound
Alt+Up / DownMove the track, renumbering every reference
Right-click a trackThe same, plus MUTATE
M, S on a TRACKS rowMute; solo (stays until clicked again)

Mixer

EXPANDED, MIXER8 strips on TRACKS; the FM-1 screen is the track's inspector
Fader, sends, PANDrag or wheel; double-click: default
MIXER (FM-1 size): click a word / right-clickMute / solo
F#3 G#3 A#3 C#4Mute T1 to T4
F3 G3 A3 B3 (held)Solo T1 to T4
C4 D4 E4 F4ALL, HOLD, FILL, TAP
F#4 G#4 A#4 C#5Mute T5 to T8
G4 A4 B4 C5 (held)Solo T5 to T8

Trying things

Right-click > LOCKA padlock: DICE and MUTATE leave it alone
Ctrl+RDICE THE PAGE (up to 40% around the defaults)
Ctrl+Shift+RMUTATE the track's sound (up to 8%)
PUT BACKUndoes the last DICE or MUTATE
A, B (EXPANDED header)Switch snapshot; right-click: store here
MORPHDrag between A and B: every setting blends
GENERATEA new piece; hold: swap with the one before

Window, files and the DAW

Click the Tessera markThe menu: SKIN, INK, MODE, SIZE, FM-1 USB, KEYS, LISTEN, TRACK, OUTPUTS, MANUAL
Drag the window's cornerAny size from 75%
Ctrl+O, Ctrl+SOPEN, SAVE (standalone; the DAW's in a DAW)
MIDI buttonClick: saves the clip; drag: onto a DAW track
Right-click a button or keyLatches it (GLO + a key with one mouse)
Program Change 0 / 1 / 2CHAIN to section A / B / C at the next phrase
Program Change 8 to 15Every track to pattern bank 1 to 8
Space (standalone)PLAY / STOP
